Register a service

Registering creates a tenant named after your service and makes you its administrator. The service does not have to be running yet — if the catalog URL can't be reached it is registered from what you type here. Its endpoints stay as you list them until you change them, or re-read them from its catalog once it is online.

Tenant

Tenants & services

Every service on this gateway is a tenant: one row holds its routes, its roles, its policies and its users. Expand a row to manage all of it, or hand it an owner and let their team run it themselves.

The service does not have to be running — an unreachable catalog is a warning, not a failure.

Platform accounts

Identities are platform-wide — one account reaches every tenant it has been granted a role in. Tenant membership is managed inside each tenant above.
